# Sykon ABS catalogue copy

The audience is architects, façade consultants, fabricators and developers
specifying systems for GCC projects. They are reading to decide whether a system
clears a requirement. Write for that.

## Voice

Engineering-led. The master's own closing line is the brief: **Engineered.
Precise. Forward.**

- State the specification. "Uf from 1.3 W/m²K" beats "excellent thermal
  performance."
- Claims carry their standard. "Class 4, EN 12207", not "highly airtight."
- German engineering is heritage, not an adjective to lean on. Say it once, in
  the tagline, where it belongs.
- Sentence case for headlines. All-caps is reserved for labels and eyebrows.

## Non-negotiables

1. **"German Engineering System Since 1967."** Keep "System". The master drops
   it in a couple of places; those are the outliers, not the rule.
2. **Display headlines end with a full stop.** "Performance with Profile."
   Taken from the master. It is a signature.
3. **Contact block is fixed**: `+971 50 804 0985`, `info@sykon.ae`,
   `www.sykon.ae`. Lowercase URL, wide space between items, never a pipe.
4. **Never claim a certification the systems do not hold.** Certification copy
   is the one place where an overstatement has legal weight. If a mark is not
   confirmed, leave it out and flag it.

## Avoid

Marketing filler that a specifier will skim past, and the tells that make copy
read as machine-written:

- "cutting-edge", "state-of-the-art", "world-class", "seamlessly", "robust
  solutions", "unlock", "elevate", "delve"
- "not just X, but Y" and "it's not about X — it's about Y"
- Rule-of-three lists used as decoration ("faster, smarter, stronger")
- Opening a paragraph by restating the heading
- Em-dash-heavy sentences stacked back to back

## Lengths

| Slot | Target |
|---|---|
| Cover headline | 12–20 words, one sentence |
| Cover deck | 15–25 words |
| Page heading | 3–8 words |
| Intro lead | 25–40 words |
| Intro column | 50–90 words |
| Pillar title | 2–4 words |
| Pillar body | 18–30 words |
| System intro | 20–35 words |
| Highlight item | 6–12 words |
| Family note | 8–14 words |

Overrun does not wrap onto a second page — pages are fixed-height with overflow
hidden, so long copy is silently clipped. `verify_layout.py` will not catch
that. Stay inside the targets, and look at the rendered page.

## British English

"façade" with the cedilla, "aluminium", "standardised", "metre", "colour". The
bundled font covers ç, é and the rest of latin-1, and ligatures are on.
